# Common tansy

*Tanacetum vulgare*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Toxic ingestion; contact dermatitis.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Common tansy is not tansy ragwort (Jacobaea vulgaris). Their chemical hazards and flower structures differ.

## Recognize

Fern-like leaves and flat clusters of yellow, button-like flowerheads without showy ray petals. [N-tanacetum-vulgare](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/tanacetum-vulgare/)

## Plants & habitat

Seeds, rhizomes and root fragments build dense stands that displace other plants and degrade pastures. [N-tanacetum-vulgare](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/tanacetum-vulgare/)

## People

Thujone can cause serious poisoning, including convulsions, and the plant can irritate skin. Avoid teas and home remedies. [N-tanacetum-vulgare](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/tanacetum-vulgare/)

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Other animals

Toxicity is reported for horses and cattle. Specialist grazing by other animals is not a general feeding recommendation. [N-tanacetum-vulgare](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/tanacetum-vulgare/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
