# Bulbous bluegrass

*Poa bulbosa*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C — Manage widespread weeds.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

Widespread and well established. Control is recommended by the state; local governments may require management. This is a management category, separate from danger ratings. [Category source](https://fremontcountyco.gov/weed-control/weed-identification) · [What the lists mean]({{BASE}}/safety/#weed-lists).

## Recognize

A small cool-season perennial with swollen stem bases and purple-green bulbils replacing many flowers in the seedhead. [U-bulbous](https://extension.usu.edu/rangeplants/grasses-and-grasslikes/bulbous-bluegrass)

## Plants & habitat

Grows early and becomes dormant as soils dry. It favors disturbed sites; the source does not describe it as equally aggressive in vigorous native vegetation. [U-bulbous](https://extension.usu.edu/rangeplants/grasses-and-grasslikes/bulbous-bluegrass)
