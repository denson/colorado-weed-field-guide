# Perennial pepperweed

*Lepidium latifolium*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Deep roots form persistent wetland colonies.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Recognize

Broad, waxy, alternate leaves and dense clusters of small white flowers on an upright perennial. [J-perennial-pepperweed](https://www.jeffco.us/2334/Perennial-Pepperweed)

## Plants & habitat

Invades wetlands, ditches and pastures. Deep and lateral roots produce new shoots; broken roots can spread an infestation. [J-perennial-pepperweed](https://www.jeffco.us/2334/Perennial-Pepperweed)
