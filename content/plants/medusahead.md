# Medusahead

*Taeniatherum caput-medusae*

**Colorado noxious weed · List A.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Barbed awns; persistent fire-promoting thatch.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Similar-looking native grasses exist. Awn and spike structure must be examined before identification or removal.

## Recognize

A winter annual grass with dense seed spikes and long, twisting, barbed awns. [WA-medusahead](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/medusahead)

## Plants & habitat

Competes strongly with native vegetation. Slowly decomposing, silica-rich thatch accumulates and can increase fire frequency. [WA-medusahead](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/medusahead)

## People

Barbed awns catch on clothing and can scratch; avoid bare-handed handling of mature seedheads. [WA-medusahead](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/medusahead)

## Dogs & cats

Awns pose a plausible physical injury concern, but this source is not a species-specific pet case series. Keep pets out of mature seedheads. [WA-medusahead](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/medusahead)

## Other animals

Poor forage quality and loss of desirable pasture are documented concerns. An unrestricted feeding-safety threshold is not established. [WA-medusahead](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/medusahead)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)

This is a statewide eradication priority. Contact your county weed program promptly for identification and an approved management and disposal plan. [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)
