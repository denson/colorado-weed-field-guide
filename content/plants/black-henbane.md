# Black henbane

*Hyoscyamus niger*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Highly poisonous; keep animals away.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Recognize

A coarse, hairy annual or biennial with sticky-looking leaves and yellowish flowers marked by purple veins. [N-hyoscyamus-niger](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hyoscyamus-niger/)

## Plants & habitat

Self-seeding produces persistent infestations on disturbed ground. Prevent mature fruits from scattering seed. [N-hyoscyamus-niger](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hyoscyamus-niger/)

## People

Tropane alkaloids can cause severe poisoning, including convulsions and coma. Do not taste or use it as a home remedy. [N-hyoscyamus-niger](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hyoscyamus-niger/)

## Dogs & cats

NC State flags dogs and cats. Treat suspected ingestion as urgent and contact a veterinarian. [N-hyoscyamus-niger](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hyoscyamus-niger/)

## Other animals

The source reports particular seed toxicity to pigs and flags horses. Animal avoidance is not a guarantee against poisoning. [N-hyoscyamus-niger](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hyoscyamus-niger/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
