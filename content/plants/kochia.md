# Kochia

*Bassia scoparia*

**Introduced annual weed; not designated on the reviewed Colorado A/B/C lists.** [C-tumbleweeds](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/identification-and-management-of-kochia-and-russian-thistle/) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Tumbling seed spread; livestock poisoning risk.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Bassia scoparia is also published as Kochia scoparia. It is distinct from Russian thistle and from the woody ornamental Euonymus alatus, also called burning bush.

## Taxonomy

Cornell uses Bassia scoparia and identifies Kochia scoparia as its synonym. [K-kochia](https://cals.cornell.edu/weed-science/weed-profiles/kochia)

## Recognize

A bushy annual with narrow, alternate, hairy leaves and small inconspicuous flowers. Plants may turn reddish and break off at the base when dry. [C-tumbleweeds](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/identification-and-management-of-kochia-and-russian-thistle/)

## Plants & habitat

Colonizes disturbed yards, roadsides and pastures. New seedlings can emerge over a long season; preventing seed set requires follow-up. Dry plants can tumble and scatter seeds. [C-tumbleweeds](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/identification-and-management-of-kochia-and-russian-thistle/)

## People

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for people. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Other animals

CSU reports several toxic compounds, including nitrates and oxalates, that can poison cattle and sheep. Maturity and drought can increase the risk. Obtain veterinary and forage-testing advice before grazing or feeding contaminated material. [C-tumbleweeds](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/identification-and-management-of-kochia-and-russian-thistle/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
