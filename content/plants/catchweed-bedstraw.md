## Identification

A weak-stemmed annual that climbs through other plants. Narrow leaves occur in whorls, typically six to eight; stems and fruits carry hooked hairs. Tiny flowers are pale and four-petaled. [N-galium-aparine](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/galium-aparine/)

## People

Scratchy stems and burrs catch on clothing and skin. Gloves are a practical handling precaution. [N-galium-aparine](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/galium-aparine/)

*Evidence scope: Mechanical-contact precaution.*

## Dogs & cats

Bristly fruits attach to fur. Check animals after moving through patches; the source does not establish pet ingestion toxicity or a frequency of injury. [N-galium-aparine](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/galium-aparine/)

*Evidence scope: Mechanical-contact precaution; clinical evidence unresolved.*

## Other animals

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for livestock. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Plants & habitat

It scrambles through vegetation in woods, floodplains and disturbed sites. CNHP treats it as native in Colorado; other sources discuss uncertainty over its broader origin. [N-galium-aparine](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/galium-aparine/) [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf)

## Management & timing

Pull young unwanted plants before flowering. Fragile stems can break and leave roots behind, so check for regrowth. [N-galium-aparine](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/galium-aparine/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
