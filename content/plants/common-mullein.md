# Common mullein

*Verbascum thapsus*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Dense rosettes and persistent volunteers.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Soft leaves do not establish medicinal or ingestion safety. This is not lamb’s-ear (Stachys byzantina).

## Recognize

A woolly leaf rosette produces a tall, dense spike of yellow flowers, typically in its second year. [N-verbascum-thapsus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/verbascum-thapsus/)

## Plants & habitat

Self-seeds in dry, disturbed places and can crowd neighboring vegetation. Prevent seed formation where control is needed. [N-verbascum-thapsus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/verbascum-thapsus/)
