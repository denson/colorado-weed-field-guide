# Curlycup gumweed

*Grindelia squarrosa*

**Native Colorado plant · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Sticky resin and conditional selenium concern.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Grindelia squarrosa includes multiple varieties. Photographs and this profile do not constitute a variety-level key or a selenium test.

## Identification

A branching, taprooted biennial or perennial with resinous foliage and flower heads. Growth form varies among the varieties grouped in this species. [F-grindelia](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/grisqu)

## People

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for people. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Dogs & cats

Sticky secretions are a practical grooming concern, inferred from reports of livestock coats becoming gummed. Pet ingestion toxicity is unresolved. [F-grindelia](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/grisqu)

*Evidence scope: Contact precaution inferred from livestock observations.*

## Other animals

Usually unpalatable to livestock. Resin can gum coats, and the plant can absorb selenium when it is available; this does not mean every specimen contains a toxic concentration. [F-grindelia](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/grisqu)

## Plants & habitat

It tolerates dry disturbed ground and may form dense patches on depleted range or roadsides. Native status and weediness depend on context. [F-grindelia](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/grisqu)

## Management & timing

Investigate site condition before broad removal. For an unwanted small patch, prevent seed production and monitor replacement vegetation. [F-grindelia](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/grisqu) [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)

*Evidence scope: Management synthesis.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
