## Recognize

Aromatic, silver-gray, finely divided leaves and small, drooping yellow-green flowerheads on a perennial. [N-artemisia-absinthium](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/artemisia-absinthium/)

## Plants & habitat

Escapes cultivation into disturbed sites and spreads by seed. Colorado’s designation applies despite ornamental or historical beverage uses. [N-artemisia-absinthium](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/artemisia-absinthium/)

## People

Thujone-containing plant material can cause serious poisoning when overused; contact dermatitis is also reported. This guide does not recommend medicinal or food preparation. [N-artemisia-absinthium](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/artemisia-absinthium/)
