# Common lambsquarters

*Chenopodium album*

**Common weed; not an A/B/C listing in the reviewed state rule.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Prolific garden weed; ingestion caution.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Chenopodium album is distinct from netseed lambsquarters (C. berlandieri). Exact native status within this variable complex is not resolved here.

## Recognize

An annual with variable goosefoot-shaped leaves, a whitish mealy coating on young growth and small green flower clusters. [N-chenopodium-album](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/chenopodium-album/)

## Plants & habitat

Pueblo County Extension identifies C. album among common Colorado weeds. Many persistent seeds allow repeated emergence after disturbance. [C-edible-weeds](https://pueblo.extension.colostate.edu/early-spring-edible-plants-in-your-yard-and-garden/) [N-chenopodium-album](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/chenopodium-album/)

## People

NC State reports digestive and nervous-system effects after large ingestion and notes oxalic acid and saponins. Food-use records are not a safe-dose recommendation. [N-chenopodium-album](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/chenopodium-album/)
