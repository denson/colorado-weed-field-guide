# Cypress spurge

*Euphorbia cyparissias*

**Colorado noxious weed · List A.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Sap can injure skin and eyes.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

Source-identified reference photographs; these are not a complete identification key. A source location outside Colorado does not establish local occurrence.

{{GALLERY}}

## Recognize

A low perennial with crowded, needle-like leaves and yellow-green flower bracts. Do not snap stems to test for milky sap. [N-euphorbia-cyparissias](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-cyparissias/)

## Plants & habitat

Rhizomes and seed build dense colonies that can overrun neighboring vegetation. [N-euphorbia-cyparissias](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-cyparissias/)

## People

Latex can cause dermatitis, mouth injury and serious corneal damage. Protect skin and eyes during handling. [N-euphorbia-cyparissias](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-cyparissias/)

## Dogs & cats

NC State flags dogs and cats and advises keeping pets away. This is not a pet-specific dose assessment. [N-euphorbia-cyparissias](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-cyparissias/)

## Other animals

The source reports potential toxicity to cattle and horses. [N-euphorbia-cyparissias](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-cyparissias/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)

This is a statewide eradication priority. Contact your county weed program promptly for identification and an approved management and disposal plan. [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)
