# Common burdock

*Arctium minus*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C — Manage widespread weeds.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

Widespread and well established. Control is recommended by the state; local governments may require management. This is a management category, separate from danger ratings. [Category source](https://fremontcountyco.gov/weed-control/weed-identification) · [What the lists mean]({{BASE}}/safety/#weed-lists).

> **Burs and fine bristles injure animals.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Recognize

A large-leaved biennial with purple flowerheads that mature into hooked burs. Leaf undersides are woolly. [N-arctium-minus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/arctium-minus/)

## Plants & habitat

Burs hitchhike on animals and clothing; persistent seeds and deep taproots make established patches difficult to remove. [N-arctium-minus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/arctium-minus/)

## People

Burs and tiny bristles can irritate eyes, skin and airways. Avoid breaking apart dry burs near the face. [N-arctium-minus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/arctium-minus/)

## Dogs & cats

The source specifically reports eye, skin and respiratory injury in dogs. Cats can also contact burs, but cat-specific outcomes were not assessed. [N-arctium-minus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/arctium-minus/)

## Other animals

Bristles can injure horses and livestock; burs can entangle small wildlife. [N-arctium-minus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/arctium-minus/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
