# African rue

*Peganum harmala*

**Colorado noxious weed · List A — Eradicate.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

Uncommon in Colorado. All populations are designated for eradication statewide. This is a management category, separate from danger ratings. [Category source](https://fremontcountyco.gov/weed-control/weed-identification) · [What the lists mean]({{BASE}}/safety/#weed-lists).

> **Highly poisonous if eaten; deep-rooted invader.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Recognize

Bright green, branching stems carry divided, narrow leaves and small white flowers with five petals. [NPS-african](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/learn/nature/africanrue.htm)

## Plants & habitat

Deep roots, drought tolerance and plentiful seeds allow invasion of disturbed dry ground. Root fragments can sprout. [NPS-african](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/learn/nature/africanrue.htm) [WA-african-rue](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/african-rue)

## People

The National Park Service describes ingestion as highly toxic. Do not taste or use this plant as a home remedy. [NPS-african](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/learn/nature/africanrue.htm)

## Dogs & cats

The source warns of animal poisoning broadly; it supplies no dog- or cat-specific dose. Keep pets from eating plants or seeds. [NPS-african](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/learn/nature/africanrue.htm)

*Evidence scope: General animal warning; pet thresholds unknown.*

## Other animals

Ingestion is a serious animal-poisoning concern. Seek veterinary advice after suspected exposure. [NPS-african](https://www.nps.gov/whsa/learn/nature/africanrue.htm)

*Evidence scope: General animal warning.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)

This is a statewide eradication priority. Contact your county weed program promptly for identification and an approved management and disposal plan. [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)
