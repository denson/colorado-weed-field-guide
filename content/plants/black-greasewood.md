# Black greasewood

*Sarcobatus vermiculatus*

**Native Colorado plant · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Oxalate poisoning can be fatal to livestock.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Greasewood is also a common name for unrelated shrubs. This account is Sarcobatus vermiculatus, not creosote bush. Livestock treatment and adaptation schedules in older sources are not reproduced.

## Identification

A woody shrub of saline ground with stiff, thorn-tipped branches, narrow fleshy leaves and small pale flowers. [A-greasewood](https://www.ars.usda.gov/pacific-west-area/logan-ut/poisonous-plant-research/docs/greasewood-sarcobatus-vermiculatus/)

## People

Thorny branches are a contact hazard. Human ingestion safety has not been established in the reviewed material. [A-greasewood](https://www.ars.usda.gov/pacific-west-area/logan-ut/poisonous-plant-research/docs/greasewood-sarcobatus-vermiculatus/)

*Evidence scope: Handling precaution inferred from structure.*

## Dogs & cats

Avoid contact with sharp branches. Livestock oxalate findings do not establish a pet-specific dose or clinical outcome. [A-greasewood](https://www.ars.usda.gov/pacific-west-area/logan-ut/poisonous-plant-research/docs/greasewood-sarcobatus-vermiculatus/)

*Evidence scope: Contact precaution; pet toxicology unresolved.*

## Other animals

Rapid heavy consumption can cause fatal oxalate poisoning in sheep and cattle. Risk varies with season, plant chemistry and other forage; do not use this page to calculate a safe ration. [A-greasewood](https://www.ars.usda.gov/pacific-west-area/logan-ut/poisonous-plant-research/docs/greasewood-sarcobatus-vermiculatus/)

## Plants & habitat

A native component of saline basins, it provides wildlife cover and browse. Dense stands require ecological assessment, not automatic treatment as an introduced invader. [F-sarcobatus](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/sarver)

## Management & timing

Ask a range specialist to assess forage access and the saline site before treatment. Existing browse value does not justify feeding an untested amount. [A-greasewood](https://www.ars.usda.gov/pacific-west-area/logan-ut/poisonous-plant-research/docs/greasewood-sarcobatus-vermiculatus/) [F-sarcobatus](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/sarver)

*Evidence scope: Management synthesis.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
