## Recognize

Yellow spurred flowers and variable leaves may combine parental traits; leaf width and flower color alone cannot confirm a hybrid. [F-toadflaxes](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-A13-PURL-gpo87594/pdf/GOVPUB-A13-PURL-gpo87594.pdf)

## Plants & habitat

Perennial hybrids spread through root buds and seeds. The federal guide reports vigorous growth and competitive potential in disturbed habitats. [F-toadflaxes](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-A13-PURL-gpo87594/pdf/GOVPUB-A13-PURL-gpo87594.pdf)
