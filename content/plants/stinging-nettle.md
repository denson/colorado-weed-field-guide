## Identification

A rhizomatous perennial with opposite, toothed leaves, small greenish flower clusters and stinging hairs. It often forms dense patches in moist, fertile ground. [N-urtica-dioica](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/urtica-dioica/) [F-urtica](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/urtdio)

*Evidence scope: Broad Urtica dioica complex.*

## People

Contact with stinging hairs can cause burning and itching. Avoid testing identification by touching the plant. [N-urtica-dioica](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/urtica-dioica/)

*Evidence scope: Broad Urtica dioica complex.*

## Dogs & cats

Keep faces and exposed skin away from stinging vegetation. This is a contact precaution based on the plant’s hairs; the reviewed sources do not establish pet-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure. [N-urtica-dioica](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/urtica-dioica/)

*Evidence scope: Precautionary inference; pet evidence unresolved.*

## Other animals

Fresh nettles are generally unpalatable to livestock. Reports of processed nettle forage do not establish that standing plants or home preparations are safe for every animal. [F-urtica](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/urtdio)

*Evidence scope: Broad-complex forage evidence.*

## Plants & habitat

Patches can crowd a small garden yet provide cover and support insects in suitable habitat. Soil disturbance can redistribute rhizomes. [F-urtica](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/urtdio) [N-urtica-dioica](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/urtica-dioica/)

*Evidence scope: Broad Urtica dioica complex.*

## Management & timing

Where removal is necessary, protect against contact and plan follow-up for rhizome regrowth. Mowing can produce new shoots rather than eliminate the patch. [F-urtica](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/urtdio)

*Evidence scope: Broad-complex management.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
