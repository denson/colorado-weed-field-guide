# Meadow death camas

*Toxicoscordion venenosum*

**Native regional plant; death-camas poisoning hazard.** [B-deathcamas](https://burkeherbarium.org/imagecollection/taxon.php?Taxon=Toxicoscordion+venenosum+var.+gramineum) [C-routt-guide](https://routt.extension.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2021/06/RC_Weed_Guide_2021.pdf)

> **Potentially fatal ingestion; onion-like bulbs.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Older sources use Zigadenus. T. venenosum includes named varieties; Colorado’s older county guide treats death camas as a group. Images show the species broadly and do not prove a particular variety or county occurrence.

## Recognize

Death camas has grass-like leaves, underground bulbs and clusters of pale yellow-white flowers. Do not taste a bulb or use onion-like appearance as an edible-plant test. [ARS-deathcamas](https://www.ars.usda.gov/Services/docs.htm?docid=9861)

*Evidence scope: Death-camas group morphology.*

## Plants & habitat

The regional taxon T. venenosum var. gramineum occurs in grassland and pine openings. Death camas is an early-season grazing hazard. [B-deathcamas](https://burkeherbarium.org/imagecollection/taxon.php?Taxon=Toxicoscordion+venenosum+var.+gramineum) [ARS-deathcamas](https://www.ars.usda.gov/Services/docs.htm?docid=9861)

*Evidence scope: Regional variety and death-camas group.*

## People

Death-camas bulbs have caused severe and fatal human poisoning. Suspected ingestion requires emergency medical help. [ARS-deathcamas](https://www.ars.usda.gov/Services/docs.htm?docid=9861)

*Evidence scope: Death-camas group including Z. venenosus.*

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Other animals

All parts contain poisonous alkaloids; cardiovascular failure can be fatal. Sheep are often affected, but cattle and horses are also at risk. [ARS-deathcamas](https://www.ars.usda.gov/Services/docs.htm?docid=9861)

*Evidence scope: Death-camas group including Z. venenosus.*
