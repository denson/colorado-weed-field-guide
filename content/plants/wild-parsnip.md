# Wild parsnip

*Pastinaca sativa*

**Common introduced weed · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Sap plus sunlight can cause severe skin burns.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Garden parsnip cultivars and wild plants belong to the same species. Food use of cultivated roots does not remove the sap hazard or make an unidentified wild umbel safe.

## Identification

A biennial with a first-year leaf rosette and later tall, grooved stems bearing yellow flower umbels. Leaves are compound with broad, toothed leaflets. [N-pastinaca-sativa](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/pastinaca-sativa/)

## People

Furocoumarins can make contacted skin sensitive to sunlight, causing burns and blisters. Avoid sap; wear gloves, long sleeves and long trousers when working near it. [N-pastinaca-sativa](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/pastinaca-sativa/)

## Dogs & cats

NC State flags this species as a problem for dogs and cats. Keep pets away from damaged plants and sap; pet-specific exposure amounts and clinical outcomes remain unresolved in the reviewed source. [N-pastinaca-sativa](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/pastinaca-sativa/)

## Other animals

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for livestock. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Plants & habitat

An introduced plant of open sites and roadsides, it can reseed into unwanted patches. Its Colorado presence is recorded by CNHP. [N-pastinaca-sativa](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/pastinaca-sativa/) [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf)

## Management & timing

Prevent seed formation only with protection against sap contact. Identification and a site-specific plan come before mowing or cutting a large patch. [N-pastinaca-sativa](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/pastinaca-sativa/)

*Evidence scope: Management synthesis from seed spread and contact hazard.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
