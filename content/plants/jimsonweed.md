# Jimsonweed

*Datura stramonium*

**Common weed; not an A/B/C listing in the reviewed state rule.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Serious poisoning risk to people and animals.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** This is Datura stramonium, not a generic profile for every Datura or Brugmansia. Photograph identification does not establish a safe exposure.

## Recognize

A coarse annual with toothed leaves, upright trumpet-shaped flowers and prickly seed capsules. [N-datura-stramonium](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/datura-stramonium/)

## Plants & habitat

A disturbed-ground weed included in Routt County’s guide. Seed contamination is a concern around animal areas. [C-routt-guide](https://routt.extension.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2021/06/RC_Weed_Guide_2021.pdf)

## People

All parts contain toxic tropane alkaloids. Ingestion can cause delirium, convulsions, coma or death; suspected ingestion needs urgent professional help. [N-datura-stramonium](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/datura-stramonium/)

## Dogs & cats

NC State flags both dogs and cats. Keep pets from chewing any part, including seeds; suspected ingestion warrants prompt veterinary contact. [N-datura-stramonium](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/datura-stramonium/)

## Other animals

Livestock poisoning is also documented. Do not allow access to contaminated feed or plants. [N-datura-stramonium](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/datura-stramonium/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
