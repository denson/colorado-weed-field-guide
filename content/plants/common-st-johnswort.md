# Common St. Johnswort

*Hypericum perforatum*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Photosensitivity and animal exposure concerns.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

This is a sourced working profile. Evidence is incomplete for **Dogs & cats**. Independent botanical and veterinary review is pending. **Uncertainty is not a finding that the plant is safe.**

## Recognize

Yellow five-petaled flowers with many stamens; small opposite leaves show translucent dots when backlit. [N-hypericum-perforatum](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hypericum-perforatum/)

## Plants & habitat

Seeds and spreading underground stems create persistent patches that can displace native plants. [N-hypericum-perforatum](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hypericum-perforatum/)

## People

Hypericin-containing plant material can cause photosensitivity and dermatitis. The guide does not assess herbal use or medication interactions. [N-hypericum-perforatum](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hypericum-perforatum/)

## Dogs & cats

NC State flags dogs and cats. This source does not establish a pet-specific safe amount. [N-hypericum-perforatum](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hypericum-perforatum/)

## Other animals

Horses are also flagged by the source. Seek veterinary advice before allowing access to contaminated forage. [N-hypericum-perforatum](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hypericum-perforatum/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
