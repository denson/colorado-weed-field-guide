# Spotted knapweed

*Centaurea stoebe*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [J-spotted-knapweed](https://www.jeffco.us/2181/Spotted-Knapweed)

> **Competes with native vegetation.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Some Colorado references use Centaurea maculosa for spotted knapweed. We retain their name in the citation record and use C. stoebe as the catalog label.

## Recognize

Purple flowerheads with dark-tipped, fringed bracts; a short-lived perennial spreading mainly by seed. [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/)

## Plants & habitat

An introduced noxious weed. Preventing seed production and maintaining desirable vegetation are parts of long-term control. [J-spotted-knapweed](https://www.jeffco.us/2181/Spotted-Knapweed)
