# Common dandelion

*Taraxacum officinale*

**Common weed; not an A/B/C listing in the reviewed state rule.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Persistent lawn volunteer; sap can irritate skin.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Used in the common broad sense (T. officinale aggregate / section Ruderalia). Photographs are not intended to resolve small segregate taxa or Colorado’s native dandelions.

## Recognize

A basal rosette of toothed leaves, single yellow heads on hollow leafless stalks, and a rounded puffball of windborne seeds. [N-taraxacum-officinale](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/taraxacum-officinale/)

## Plants & habitat

A familiar Colorado lawn plant with a persistent taproot. Flowers provide early pollen and nectar; removal is a site choice, not a claim that it has no ecological value. [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/) [N-taraxacum-officinale](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/taraxacum-officinale/)

## People

Sap can cause contact dermatitis in susceptible people. Recorded food uses do not establish that an unidentified or treated lawn plant is safe to eat. [N-taraxacum-officinale](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/taraxacum-officinale/)

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Other animals

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for livestock. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
