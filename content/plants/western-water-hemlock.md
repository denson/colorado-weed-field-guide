# Western water hemlock

*Cicuta douglasii*

**Native wetland hazard; not an A/B/C listing in the reviewed rule.** [C-wetland-guide](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/download/documents/cwic_docs/WesternSlope.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Potentially fatal poisoning; wetland look-alike danger.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** The older Colorado wetland guide treats C. douglasii within C. maculata var. angustifolia. The photos and Utah source identify C. douglasii. This is separate from poison hemlock (Conium maculatum). Older home-treatment instructions and an incorrect chemical classification in sources are excluded.

## Recognize

A wetland perennial with clusters of small white flowers and divided leaves; veins terminate in notches between the leaflet teeth. Never taste, cut roots or use this short description as a foraging test. [U-water-hemlock](https://extension.usu.edu/rangeplants/forbs-herbaceous/water-hemlock)

## Plants & habitat

Occurs around wet ground, ditches and waterways. Colorado floras have used broader Cicuta names for related populations. [C-wetland-guide](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/download/documents/cwic_docs/WesternSlope.pdf)

*Evidence scope: Older regional flora; taxonomic scope differs.*

## People

Routt County identifies water hemlock as a potentially lethal poison. Treat suspected ingestion as an emergency. [C-routt-guide](https://routt.extension.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2021/06/RC_Weed_Guide_2021.pdf)

## Dogs & cats

The county guide explicitly warns about dogs. Cat-specific clinical detail is not established here; keep all pets away from chewing plants or exposed roots. [C-routt-guide](https://routt.extension.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2021/06/RC_Weed_Guide_2021.pdf)

*Evidence scope: Dog warning; cat evidence gap.*

## Other animals

Highly poisonous to livestock, especially the roots. Convulsions and death can follow ingestion. [U-water-hemlock](https://extension.usu.edu/rangeplants/forbs-herbaceous/water-hemlock)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
