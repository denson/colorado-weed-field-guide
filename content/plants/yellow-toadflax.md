# Yellow toadflax

*Linaria vulgaris*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [J-yellow-toadflax](https://www.jeffco.us/2142/Yellow-Toadflax)

> **Spreads from roots; livestock concern.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Distinguish the two toadflaxes by leaves as well as flowers. A snapdragon-like bloom is not evidence of garden suitability.

## Recognize

Yellow flowers with orange throats and long spurs; narrow, waxy leaves. Also called butter and eggs. [J-yellow-toadflax](https://www.jeffco.us/2142/Yellow-Toadflax)

## Plants & habitat

A perennial spreading by roots and seed. Root fragments can form new plants; repeated casual digging or mowing is unlikely to solve an established patch. [J-yellow-toadflax](https://www.jeffco.us/2142/Yellow-Toadflax)

## Other animals

The county reports possible livestock toxicity. Grazing is not recommended as a simple control method; pet safety cannot be inferred from this warning. [J-yellow-toadflax](https://www.jeffco.us/2142/Yellow-Toadflax)
