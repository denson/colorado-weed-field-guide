# Yellow nutsedge

*Cyperus esculentus*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Underground tubers sustain infestations.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** This listed sedge is described as native in the NC source; that broad range label is not a Colorado nativity assessment. It is distinct from purple nutsedge (Cyperus rotundus).

## Recognize

A glossy, grass-like sedge with narrow, channeled leaves and triangular stems. Underground tubers are important identification features. [N-cyperus-esculentus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cyperus-esculentus/)

## Plants & habitat

Rhizomes and tubers compete strongly with crops and can remain after shoots are pulled. Ordinary lawn-grass treatments may not control a sedge. [N-cyperus-esculentus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cyperus-esculentus/)
