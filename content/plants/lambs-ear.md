# Lamb’s-ear

*Stachys byzantina*

**Introduced low-water perennial.** [N-stachys-byzantina](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/stachys-byzantina/)

> **Can spread; pet evidence gap.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Cultivars differ in flowering and leaf size. Deer resistance does not demonstrate safety for pets.

## Recognize

Thick, soft, woolly silver leaves form low rosettes; flowering forms carry purplish-pink spikes. [N-stachys-byzantina](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/stachys-byzantina/)

## Plants & habitat

Stems root where they touch soil. It can spread aggressively in favorable conditions; divide when needed and avoid wet soil. [N-stachys-byzantina](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/stachys-byzantina/)
