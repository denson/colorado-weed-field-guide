# Plumeless thistle

*Carduus acanthoides*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Sharp spines; pasture competition.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Distinguish introduced Carduus from Colorado’s native thistles before removal.

## Recognize

A spiny thistle with winged stems and relatively small, reddish-purple flowerheads. [J-plumeless-thistle](https://www.jeffco.us/2294/Plumeless-Thistle)

## Plants & habitat

Spreads by seed through disturbed pasture and roadsides. Follow-up is needed as rosettes germinate. [J-plumeless-thistle](https://www.jeffco.us/2294/Plumeless-Thistle)

## People

Spines on leaves, stems and flowerhead bracts can puncture skin; use protective handling. [J-plumeless-thistle](https://www.jeffco.us/2294/Plumeless-Thistle)

*Evidence scope: Physical-risk inference.*

## Dogs & cats

Spines may injure mouths or paws. Pet-specific chemical toxicity is not established here. [J-plumeless-thistle](https://www.jeffco.us/2294/Plumeless-Thistle)

*Evidence scope: Physical-risk inference; ingestion evidence gap.*

## Other animals

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for livestock. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
