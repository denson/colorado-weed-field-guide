## Identification

An annual with variable leaves, a mealy underside and grooved stems; green flowers are inconspicuous. Confirm mature seed characters before separating it from common lambsquarters. [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/)

*Evidence scope: Species description; identification limitation.*

## Plants & habitat

Rapid growth and water use can make it competitive in cultivated ground. [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/)

## Management & timing

Pull or hoe young plants and prevent seed maturation where they are unwanted. [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/)
