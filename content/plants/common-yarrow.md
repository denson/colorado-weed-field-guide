# Common yarrow

*Achillea millefolium*

**Low-water garden plant.** [S03](https://www.extension.colostate.edu/docs/pubs/native/FrontRangeSm.pdf) [S07](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/achillea-millefolium/)

## Recognize

Finely divided, fernlike leaves and flat-topped clusters of small flowers. Native range includes North America, Europe, and Asia; cultivated forms vary. [S07](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/achillea-millefolium/)

## Plants & habitat

Tolerates drought and feeds pollinators, but can spread aggressively through underground stems and seed. [S07](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/achillea-millefolium/)

## People

Contact can cause dermatitis. Gloves reduce skin contact during garden work. [S07](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/achillea-millefolium/)

## Dogs & cats

Toxic to dogs and cats. Reported signs include vomiting, diarrhea, drooling, and dermatitis. [S08](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/yarrow)

## Other animals

Also toxic to horses. ASPCA includes colic among reported signs; do not infer safety for other livestock. [S08](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/yarrow)
