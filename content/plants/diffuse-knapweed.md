# Diffuse knapweed

*Centaurea diffusa*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B — Stop the spread.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

Established in some areas. Requirements vary by location: eradication, containment or suppression to stop further spread. This is a management category, separate from danger ratings. [Category source](https://fremontcountyco.gov/weed-control/weed-identification) · [What the lists mean]({{BASE}}/safety/#weed-lists).

> **Skin irritation; tumbling seed spread.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Spotted knapweed and spotted × diffuse hybrids require separate identification. Russian-knapweed horse poisoning must not be transferred merely because the common names overlap.

## Recognize

Much-branched stems carry small white to pale purple flowerheads with long terminal spines on their bracts. [J-diffuse-knapweed](https://www.jeffco.us/2466/Diffuse-Knapweed)

## Plants & habitat

Dry plants break off and tumble, spreading seeds through disturbed rangeland and roadsides. [J-diffuse-knapweed](https://www.jeffco.us/2466/Diffuse-Knapweed)

## People

The county reports skin irritation. Wear gloves when handling plants. [J-diffuse-knapweed](https://www.jeffco.us/2466/Diffuse-Knapweed)

## Dogs & cats

Spiny bracts present a physical contact concern. The reviewed source does not establish pet ingestion effects. [J-diffuse-knapweed](https://www.jeffco.us/2466/Diffuse-Knapweed)

*Evidence scope: Physical-risk inference; chemical-toxicity gap.*
