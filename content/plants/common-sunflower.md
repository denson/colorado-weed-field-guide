# Common sunflower

*Helianthus annuus*

**Colorado native.** [S28](https://pueblo.extension.colostate.edu/loving-our-native-plants/) [S23](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/helianthus-annuus/)

> **Listed non-toxic to dogs & cats.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Three views of this plant

Photos are source-identified examples, not a complete identification key. Check leaves, stems, flowers and fruit together. Locations are not assumed to be in Colorado.

{{GALLERY}}

## Recognize

An annual with rough, hairy stems and yellow flower rays around darker central disks. [S23](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/helianthus-annuus/)

## Plants & habitat

Readily self-seeds and can become weedy. Garden judgment: thin unwanted volunteers where they crowd other plants. [S23](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/helianthus-annuus/)

## People

Human ingestion safety is not evaluated in this guide. This profile is not a recommendation to forage.

## Dogs & cats

ASPCA explicitly lists common sunflower as non-toxic to dogs and cats. Any plant material can still upset a pet’s stomach. [S24](https://www.aspca.org/news/plants-support-your-local-wildlife) [S25](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants)

## Other animals

Seeds feed birds and squirrels. Livestock safety is not established by the dog-and-cat source. [S23](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/helianthus-annuus/) [S24](https://www.aspca.org/news/plants-support-your-local-wildlife)

## Before taking action

Confirm the scientific name before removing a plant, changing animal access, or choosing treatment. An evidence gap means this review has not resolved the question. It does not mean safe. For listed weeds, consult your county weed program about the requirements for your location; follow the current label for any pesticide. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
