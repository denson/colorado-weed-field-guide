# Prairie sage

*Artemisia ludoviciana*

**Colorado native.** [S03](https://www.extension.colostate.edu/docs/pubs/native/FrontRangeSm.pdf)

> **Spreading roots; pet evidence gap.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Common name white sage also refers to other plants, including Salvia apiana. Do not merge their uses or toxicology.

## Recognize

An upright silver-leaved perennial with small, inconspicuous flowerheads. [N-artemisia-ludoviciana](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/artemisia-ludoviciana/)

## Plants & habitat

Spreads by rhizomes and seed, sometimes becoming weedy. Dry, well-drained soil suits it; leave room or manage expansion at bed edges. [N-artemisia-ludoviciana](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/artemisia-ludoviciana/)

## Other animals

The Front Range guide records pollen and seed value for insects and birds. Those benefits do not establish livestock or pet safety. [S03](https://www.extension.colostate.edu/docs/pubs/native/FrontRangeSm.pdf)
