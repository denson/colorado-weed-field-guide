# Perennial sowthistle

*Sonchus arvensis*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Persistent underground spread.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Annual sowthistles are separate species. A yellow flowerhead and milky sap are insufficient identification.

## Recognize

Yellow dandelion-like flowerheads on leafy stalks; leaves clasp the stem and may have prickly margins. [J-perennial-sowthistle](https://www.jeffco.us/2323/Perennial-Sowthistle)

## Plants & habitat

A perennial of moist disturbed places with spreading underground growth. Seed prevention and repeated follow-up are needed. [J-perennial-sowthistle](https://www.jeffco.us/2323/Perennial-Sowthistle)
