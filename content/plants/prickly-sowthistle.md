# Prickly sowthistle

*Sonchus asper*

**Common introduced weed · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

**Identification caveat:** This is an annual Sonchus, not a true Cirsium thistle and not perennial sowthistle. Confirm leaf bases and life cycle.

## Identification

An annual with yellow flower heads and often glossy, prickly-edged leaves. Rounded basal leaf lobes clasp the stem, unlike the more pointed lobes of annual sowthistle. [N-sonchus-asper](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/sonchus-asper/) [U-sowthistle](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/weeds-identification-gallery/annual-sowthistle/)

## People

Spiny leaf edges warrant care when handling. No ingestion safety has been established here. [N-sonchus-asper](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/sonchus-asper/)

*Evidence scope: Handling precaution inferred from leaf structure.*

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Other animals

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for livestock. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Plants & habitat

It grows in gardens, roadsides and disturbed ground. Plants can bolt quickly, and seeds travel by wind and water. [N-sonchus-asper](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/sonchus-asper/)

## Management & timing

Inspect patches often and remove unwanted plants before fluffy seedheads form. [N-sonchus-asper](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/sonchus-asper/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
