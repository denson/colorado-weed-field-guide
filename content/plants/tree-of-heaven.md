# Tree of heaven

*Ailanthus altissima*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Root suckers form persistent thickets.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Distinguish from sumacs and walnuts using leaflet bases and other features. Established root systems require a coordinated control plan.

## Recognize

Large compound leaves have leaflets with glandular teeth near their bases; fruits are elongated, winged samaras. [N-ailanthus-altissima](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ailanthus-altissima/)

## Plants & habitat

Seeds and suckers form dense thickets that displace vegetation. It is a host for spotted lanternfly; tree presence does not prove that insect is locally present. [N-ailanthus-altissima](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ailanthus-altissima/)
