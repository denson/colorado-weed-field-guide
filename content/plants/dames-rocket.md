# Dame’s rocket

*Hesperis matronalis*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [J-dames-rocket](https://www.jeffco.us/2467/Dames-Rocket)

> **Escapes gardens and spreads.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Four petals help distinguish this mustard relative from five-petaled garden phlox; this clue alone is not a complete identification.

## Recognize

Fragrant purple, pink or white flowers with four petals; often a biennial or short-lived perennial. [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/)

## Plants & habitat

Introduced as an ornamental and can spread aggressively. Do not use seed mixes containing it; prevent seed set and follow up on seedlings. [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/)
