# Redstem filaree

*Erodium cicutarium*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Seeds spread through lawns and pasture.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** The county’s unusually large height figure is not adopted. This guide relies on flower, rosette and fruit features instead.

## Recognize

A hairy rosette plant with pink-purple flowers and long-beaked fruits that develop corkscrew-like seed tails. [J-redstem-filaree](https://www.jeffco.us/2240/Redstem-Filaree)

## Plants & habitat

An introduced annual or biennial found in gardens, turf and disturbed land. Prevent seed spread and contain seed-bearing material. [J-redstem-filaree](https://www.jeffco.us/2240/Redstem-Filaree)
