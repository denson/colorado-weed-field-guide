# Spotted × diffuse knapweed hybrid

*Centaurea × psammogena*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B — Stop the spread.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

Established in some areas. Requirements vary by location: eradication, containment or suppression to stop further spread. This is a management category, separate from danger ratings. [Category source](https://fremontcountyco.gov/weed-control/weed-identification) · [What the lists mean]({{BASE}}/safety/#weed-lists).

> **Hybrid identification needs expert review.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** The source explains chromosome and subspecies differences in the parents and uncertainty in older identifications. The photographs come from a community research-grade Colorado observation, not genetic testing. No parent-species poisoning syndrome is assumed for the hybrid.

## Recognize

A fertile hybrid of diffuse and spotted knapweed, often misidentified. A mix of parental appearance does not by itself prove hybrid identity. [F-knapweed](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/cendif)

## Plants & habitat

The Forest Service records the hybrid in Colorado. Colorado lists the hybrid separately, so do not discard suspect plants from a weed survey simply because they do not fit a parent’s key. [F-knapweed](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/cendif) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)
