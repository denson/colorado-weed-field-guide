# Western poison ivy

*Toxicodendron rydbergii*

**Colorado native; contact hazard.** [C-wetland-guide](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/download/documents/cwic_docs/WesternSlope.pdf)

> **Skin-rash oil can travel on pet fur.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** The FDA advice covers poison ivy as a group. Source identification distinguishes this western species from other Toxicodendron taxa.

## Recognize

A low shrub with leaves divided into three leaflets and pale fruits. Colorado’s regional flora identifies this native species. [C-wetland-guide](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/download/documents/cwic_docs/WesternSlope.pdf)

## Plants & habitat

Spreads through underground growth. Its native ecological role does not remove the need to manage contact near paths and play areas. [C-wetland-guide](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/download/documents/cwic_docs/WesternSlope.pdf)

*Evidence scope: Ecology plus site-management inference.*

## People

Urushiol oil can cause an itchy rash and remain on tools or clothing. Smoke from burning poison ivy is hazardous; do not burn it. [FDA-poison-ivy](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/outsmarting-poison-ivy-and-other-poisonous-plants)

*Evidence scope: Poison-ivy group evidence.*

## Dogs & cats

Most pets are less sensitive to the oil, but fur can carry it to people. Avoid barehanded contact with exposed fur and get advice on safe cleaning. [FDA-poison-ivy](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/outsmarting-poison-ivy-and-other-poisonous-plants)

*Evidence scope: Poison-ivy group evidence.*

## Other animals

The Colorado flora describes wildlife browsing. This does not establish safe feed quantities for livestock or pets. [C-wetland-guide](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/download/documents/cwic_docs/WesternSlope.pdf)

*Evidence scope: Ecological observation, not feed clearance.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
