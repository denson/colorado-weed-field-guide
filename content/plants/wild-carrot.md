# Wild carrot

*Daucus carota*

**Common introduced weed · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Do not confuse it with highly poisonous hemlocks.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Wild carrot, poison hemlock and water hemlocks require careful separation. A dark floret, a smell or a photograph by itself is insufficient. This profile does not authorize eating wild roots.

## Identification

A biennial with finely divided leaves, bristly stems and white flower umbels. These clues alone are not a safe identification key. [N-daucus-carota](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/daucus-carota/)

## People

Sap may cause dermatitis, including reactions in light. Do not taste an unidentified carrot-family plant; this guide is not a foraging identification service. [N-daucus-carota](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/daucus-carota/)

*Evidence scope: Species contact hazard; identification precaution.*

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Other animals

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for livestock. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Plants & habitat

An introduced roadside and field plant, it can spread aggressively by seed. A deep taproot can complicate pulling established plants. [N-daucus-carota](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/daucus-carota/)

## Management & timing

Confirm identity before handling. Prevent reseeding in unwanted patches while avoiding sap contact. [N-daucus-carota](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/daucus-carota/)

*Evidence scope: Management inference from reproduction and sap hazard.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
