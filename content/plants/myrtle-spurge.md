# Myrtle spurge

*Euphorbia myrsinites*

**Colorado List A.** [S14](https://www.jeffco.us/2364/Myrtle-Spurge)

> **Poisonous · Eye & skin injury.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Three views of this plant

Photos are source-identified examples, not a complete identification key. Check leaves, stems, flowers and fruit together. Locations are not assumed to be in Colorado.

{{GALLERY}}

## Recognize

Trailing fleshy stems, spiraled blue-green leaves, and yellow-green spring bracts. Previously sold as an ornamental. [S13](https://ag.colorado.gov/conservation/noxious-weeds/colorado-noxious-weed-list/myrtle-spurge) [S14](https://www.jeffco.us/2364/Myrtle-Spurge) [S15](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-myrsinites/)

## Plants & habitat

Spreads aggressively by seed. Colorado’s List A goal is eradication; do not select it for a xeriscape garden. [S13](https://ag.colorado.gov/conservation/noxious-weeds/colorado-noxious-weed-list/myrtle-spurge) [S15](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-myrsinites/) [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)

## People

Sap can cause severe dermatitis and damage the eye’s cornea. All parts are poisonous if eaten; avoid skin and eye contact. [S14](https://www.jeffco.us/2364/Myrtle-Spurge) [S15](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-myrsinites/)

## Dogs & cats

Keep pets away from the poisonous plant and its irritating sap. NC State specifically flags problems for dogs and cats. [S15](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-myrsinites/)

## Other animals

Species-specific livestock effects are not detailed in the references used here. Do not treat that evidence gap as clearance for grazing. [S14](https://www.jeffco.us/2364/Myrtle-Spurge) [S15](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-myrsinites/)

Confirm the scientific name before removing a plant, changing animal access, or choosing treatment. An evidence gap means this review has not resolved the question. It does not mean safe.
