# Giant salvinia

*Salvinia molesta*

**Colorado noxious weed · List A.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Floating mats harm aquatic habitat.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Several floating ferns look similar. Close inspection of leaf hairs is needed; no photo alone establishes identification.

## Recognize

A floating fern with paired surface fronds and submerged, root-like fronds. Magnification reveals characteristic joined, eggbeater-shaped hairs. [N-salvinia-molesta](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/salvinia-molesta/)

## Plants & habitat

Thick mats restrict light and oxygen, clog water intakes and obstruct recreation. Do not introduce it into ponds or discard aquarium material outdoors. [N-salvinia-molesta](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/salvinia-molesta/)

## People

Dense surface mats obstruct boating and swimming; this is a water-access hazard rather than a demonstrated ingestion syndrome. [N-salvinia-molesta](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/salvinia-molesta/)

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Other animals

Habitat effects include reduced oxygen and harm to fish and aquatic plants. Direct livestock toxicity is not established here. [N-salvinia-molesta](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/salvinia-molesta/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)

This is a statewide eradication priority. Contact your county weed program promptly for identification and an approved management and disposal plan. [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)
