# Big sagebrush

*Artemisia tridentata*

**Native Colorado plant · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Valuable native habitat; subspecies and pet evidence need care.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** A broad Artemisia tridentata profile, not a key to its subspecies. The current three photographs are source-identified as subsp. vaseyana (mountain big sagebrush). They do not illustrate every subspecies. Culinary sage and Russian sage belong to different taxa.

## Identification

An aromatic evergreen shrub with gray-green foliage, often with three small lobes at the leaf tip. Height, branching and flowering pattern vary substantially among subspecies. [A-big-sagebrush](https://plants.usda.gov/DocumentLibrary/plantguide/pdf/pg_artrw8.pdf)

## People

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for people. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Other animals

An important winter food and cover plant for many wildlife species. Livestock use varies with plant chemistry and subspecies; wildlife browsing is not a safe feeding recommendation for pets. [A-big-sagebrush](https://plants.usda.gov/DocumentLibrary/plantguide/pdf/pg_artrw8.pdf)

## Plants & habitat

CSU includes it among native landscape shrubs for low-water sites. Natural sagebrush habitat supports many dependent animals; an unwanted garden seedling is a different management question. [C-native-shrubs](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/native-shrubs-for-colorado-landscapes/) [A-big-sagebrush](https://plants.usda.gov/DocumentLibrary/plantguide/pdf/pg_artrw8.pdf)

## Management & timing

Match the plant and subspecies to the site and avoid excessive moisture. Consider retaining established native habitat before removing shrubs. [C-native-shrubs](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/native-shrubs-for-colorado-landscapes/) [A-big-sagebrush](https://plants.usda.gov/DocumentLibrary/plantguide/pdf/pg_artrw8.pdf)

*Evidence scope: Landscape synthesis.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
