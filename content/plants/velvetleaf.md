# Velvetleaf

*Abutilon theophrasti*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Persistent seeds and strong crop competition.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Recognize

An upright summer annual with velvety, heart-shaped leaves, yellow five-petaled flowers and a ring-like capsule of pointed segments. [K-velvetleaf](https://cals.cornell.edu/weed-science/weed-profiles/velvetleaf)

## Plants & habitat

A competitive crop weed whose durable seedbank can require years of follow-up. Prevent plants from replenishing seeds. [K-velvetleaf](https://cals.cornell.edu/weed-science/weed-profiles/velvetleaf)
