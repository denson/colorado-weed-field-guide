# Giant ragweed

*Ambrosia trifida*

**Native Colorado plant · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Allergenic pollen and strong competition in disturbed ground.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Identification

An annual with rough stems and opposite leaves, usually with three to five deep lobes. Early leaves may be unlobed. Male flower clusters stand at the stem tips. [I-giant-ragweed](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/encyclopedia/giant-ragweed)

## People

Windborne pollen is a significant hay-fever trigger for susceptible people. Colorado extension describes late-season ragweed exposure; local timing varies with weather. [I-giant-ragweed](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/encyclopedia/giant-ragweed) [C-ragweed-native](https://sam.extension.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/44/2019/08/August-31-2019-Ragweed.pdf)

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Other animals

Some animals browse the foliage; that does not establish a safe feed amount or a pet-safety rating. [I-giant-ragweed](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/encyclopedia/giant-ragweed)

*Evidence scope: Forage observation; safety limitation.*

## Plants & habitat

Early emergence and tall growth can help it dominate disturbed sites. Resistance to some herbicide groups is documented in particular populations. [I-giant-ragweed](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/encyclopedia/giant-ragweed)

## Management & timing

Remove young plants before flowering and seed formation. For a large or persistent infestation, consult a local weed specialist about identification and resistance. [C-ragweed-native](https://sam.extension.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/44/2019/08/August-31-2019-Ragweed.pdf)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
