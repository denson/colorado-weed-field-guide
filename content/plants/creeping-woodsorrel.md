# Creeping woodsorrel

*Oxalis corniculata*

**Common introduced weed · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Oxalate-containing plant; keep pets from grazing.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Oxalis corniculata is not a Trifolium clover or a Rumex sorrel. Do not attach an ASPCA Rumex scutatus entry to this plant merely because both are called sorrel.

## Identification

Three heart-shaped leaflets can resemble clover, but the flowers are yellow and seed capsules are narrow and cylindrical. Creeping stems spread across the ground. [N-oxalis-corniculata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/oxalis-corniculata/)

## People

All parts have toxic potential from soluble oxalates. A sour taste or a reference to traditional food use is not a safe-amount recommendation. [N-oxalis-corniculata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/oxalis-corniculata/)

## Dogs & cats

NC State flags this species as a problem for dogs and cats. Prevent repeated grazing; the reviewed entry does not quantify a safe amount or species-specific clinical outcome in pets. [N-oxalis-corniculata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/oxalis-corniculata/)

## Other animals

The source reports colic and possible kidney injury after substantial ingestion of Oxalis by horses. [N-oxalis-corniculata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/oxalis-corniculata/)

*Evidence scope: Genus-level horse evidence.*

## Plants & habitat

Ripe capsules eject seeds, and spreading stems can make established patches difficult to remove. [N-oxalis-corniculata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/oxalis-corniculata/)

## Management & timing

Inspect incoming nursery plants and remove volunteers before capsules mature. Recheck for regrowth and seedlings. [N-oxalis-corniculata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/oxalis-corniculata/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
