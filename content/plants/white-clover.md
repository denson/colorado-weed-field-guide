# White clover

*Trifolium repens*

**Common introduced weed · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

**Identification caveat:** White clover (Trifolium repens) differs from white sweetclover (Melilotus albus); the moldy sweetclover-hay warning is not assigned to it solely from a common name.

## Identification

A low perennial with three leaflets, often bearing pale crescents, and rounded heads of white flowers. Runners root at their joints. [N-trifolium-repens](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/trifolium-repens/)

## Other animals

It is grown as livestock forage. This botanical description is not a complete feeding-risk assessment and gives no safe ration. [N-trifolium-repens](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/trifolium-repens/)

*Evidence scope: Forage-use observation; safety limitation.*

## Plants & habitat

White clover fixes nitrogen, attracts pollinators and spreads in lawns. Whether it is a weed depends on the intended planting. [N-trifolium-repens](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/trifolium-repens/)

## Management & timing

First decide whether the clover is unwanted. Its creeping stems can tolerate mowing; manage lawn conditions if trying to favor turf. [N-trifolium-repens](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/trifolium-repens/) [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
