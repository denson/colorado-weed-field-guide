# Common chickweed

*Stellaria media*

**Common introduced weed · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **A cool-season weed that can seed while still small.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Common chickweed is not every plant called chickweed. Species identification and contamination history must be established independently of any food-use claim.

## Identification

A soft, spreading annual with opposite oval leaves and small white flowers. Five deeply split petals look like ten. A line of hairs runs along the stems. [N-stellaria-media](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/stellaria-media/)

## People

The source reports saponins in the leaves alongside food uses. This profile does not establish a safe portion or identify a plant for foraging. [N-stellaria-media](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/stellaria-media/)

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Other animals

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for livestock. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Plants & habitat

It can root at stem joints and reseed quickly in moist, fertile ground. It also provides food and habitat for insects and birds. [N-stellaria-media](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/stellaria-media/)

## Management & timing

Look for small plants during cool weather. Shallow roots make young patches relatively easy to pull; remove them before further seed production. [N-stellaria-media](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/stellaria-media/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
