## Identification

A summer annual grass with sprawling stems that root at joints, hairy leaf bases and fingerlike seedhead branches. This profile covers large crabgrass; the common name also includes other Digitaria species. [N-digitaria-sanguinalis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/digitaria-sanguinalis/) [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/)

## Plants & habitat

It colonizes disturbed soil and lawns. Its ability to root along spreading stems helps it occupy bare patches. [N-digitaria-sanguinalis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/digitaria-sanguinalis/)

## Management & timing

Remove seedlings before extensive rooting and seed production. Mulch garden beds and maintain dense turf; frost kills the annual plant, but surviving seeds can start another generation. [N-digitaria-sanguinalis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/digitaria-sanguinalis/)
