# Hoary cress

*Lepidium draba*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Spreading roots; possible livestock poisoning.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

Source-identified reference photographs; these are not a complete identification key. A source location outside Colorado does not establish local occurrence.

{{GALLERY}}

## Recognize

Blue-green leaves with clasping upper bases and crowded clusters of small white, four-petaled flowers. [J-hoary-cress](https://www.jeffco.us/2436/Hoary-Cress)

## Plants & habitat

Creeping roots create dense colonies in fields and disturbed ground. Root pieces can regrow after pulling or cultivation. [J-hoary-cress](https://www.jeffco.us/2436/Hoary-Cress)

## People

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for people. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Other animals

The county reports possible livestock poisoning and advises against grazing as a simple control method. [J-hoary-cress](https://www.jeffco.us/2436/Hoary-Cress)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
