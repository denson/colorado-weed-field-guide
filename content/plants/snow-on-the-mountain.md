# Snow-on-the-mountain

*Euphorbia marginata*

**Native Colorado plant · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Latex can damage skin and eyes; toxic if eaten.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** The same common name is used for Aegopodium podagraria, an unrelated groundcover. This profile and its sap warnings apply to Euphorbia marginata.

## Identification

An upright annual whose upper leaves develop striking white margins. Tiny flowers sit within the showy pale bracts. Damaged tissues exude milky latex. [N-euphorbia-marginata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-marginata/)

## People

Sap can cause dermatitis, mouth blistering and serious eye damage. Wear gloves and prevent sap contact, especially with eyes. [N-euphorbia-marginata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-marginata/)

## Dogs & cats

NC State flags this species as hazardous to pets. Prevent chewing and contact with latex; the source provides no safe pet exposure amount. [N-euphorbia-marginata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-marginata/)

## Other animals

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for livestock. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Plants & habitat

A native plains annual also grown as an ornamental. It tolerates dry, poor soil and can reseed into unwanted patches. [N-euphorbia-marginata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-marginata/)

## Management & timing

Handle with protection and remove unwanted plants before seed maturation. Keep freshly cut material inaccessible to children and animals. [N-euphorbia-marginata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-marginata/)

*Evidence scope: Management synthesis.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
