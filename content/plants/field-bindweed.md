# Field bindweed

*Convolvulus arvensis*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C.** [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/)

> **Smothers plants; livestock concern.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Morning glory is an ambiguous common name. Ipomoea poison listings do not automatically apply to Convolvulus arvensis.

## Three views of this plant

Photos are source-identified examples, not a complete identification key. Check leaves, stems, flowers and fruit together. Locations are not assumed to be in Colorado.

{{GALLERY}}

## Recognize

Twining or trailing vines with arrowhead leaves and white to pink funnel-shaped flowers. [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/)

## Plants & habitat

Deep roots and persistent seed make control difficult. Vines overgrow neighboring plants; repeated shoot removal and a site-specific plan are usually needed. [N-convolvulus-arvensis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/convolvulus-arvensis/) [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/)

## People

This review did not establish human ingestion effects. This is an evidence gap, not proof of safety.

## Dogs & cats

Species-specific ingestion effects and exposure thresholds were not established in the reviewed sources. This is an evidence gap, not proof of safety.

## Other animals

Cornell reports alkaloids that can cause digestive problems in some livestock. Pet effects and a safe feeding threshold are not established here. [K-bindweed](https://blogs.cornell.edu/weedid/859-2/)

## Before taking action

Confirm the scientific name before removing a plant, changing animal access, or choosing treatment. An evidence gap means this review has not resolved the question. It does not mean safe. For listed weeds, consult your county weed program about the requirements for your location; follow the current label for any pesticide. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
