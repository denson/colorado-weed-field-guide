*Cynoglossum officinale*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [J-houndstongue](https://www.jeffco.us/2414/Houndstongue)

> **Toxic to livestock.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Recognize

Hairy leaves form a rosette before stems carry small reddish-purple flowers. A biennial or short-lived perennial. [J-houndstongue](https://www.jeffco.us/2414/Houndstongue)

## Plants & habitat

Introduced from Eurasia; spreads by seed in disturbed rangelands and pastures. Prevent seed production and monitor rosettes. [J-houndstongue](https://www.jeffco.us/2414/Houndstongue)

## Other animals

Contains alkaloids poisonous to livestock. The county explicitly advises against grazing it. [J-houndstongue](https://www.jeffco.us/2414/Houndstongue)
