# Blanketflower

*Gaillardia aristata*

**Colorado native; low-water garden plant.** [C-perennials](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/native-herbaceous-perennials-for-colorado-landscapes/)

> **Contact dermatitis possible.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Many nursery blanketflowers are Gaillardia × grandiflora hybrids. Their appearance does not prove they are G. aristata.

## Recognize

Daisy-like heads with yellow or reddish ray flowers and darker centers; leaves and stems have hairs. [N-gaillardia-aristata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/gaillardia-aristata/)

## Plants & habitat

Suited to hot, dry sites with drainage; seed and division propagate it. Flowers support pollinators and seedheads attract birds. Garden judgment: remove unwanted volunteers around smaller plants. [N-gaillardia-aristata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/gaillardia-aristata/)

## People

Contact dermatitis is reported; wear gloves if sensitive when handling plants. [N-gaillardia-aristata](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/gaillardia-aristata/)
