# Quackgrass

*Elymus repens*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Rhizome fragments regrow in lawns and gardens.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Recognize

A perennial grass with clasping auricles at the leaf collar and pale underground rhizomes with sharp tips. [M-quackgrass](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/lawns-and-landscapes-in-minnesota/quackgrass)

## Plants & habitat

Rhizomes compete with desired vegetation and regrow from pieces. Repeated disturbance without removing or exhausting them can spread the patch. [M-quackgrass](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/lawns-and-landscapes-in-minnesota/quackgrass)
