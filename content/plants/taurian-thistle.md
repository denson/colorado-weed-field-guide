# Taurian thistle

*Onopordum tauricum*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Sharp spines; taxon listed as Scotch thistle.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Onopordum acanthium has its own profile. Identification can be difficult; the reference study documents morphological variation among Italian populations, not Colorado impact severity.

## Recognize

Tall, spiny, winged stems and glandular hairs; often greener and less woolly than O. acanthium. [P-tauricum](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10934427/)

## Plants & habitat

Colorado lists O. tauricum as a separate List B entry under the common name Scotch thistle. Confirm the species with your county program. [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

*Evidence scope: Regulatory status.*

## People

Spiny stems and leaves warrant protective handling. [P-tauricum](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10934427/)

*Evidence scope: Physical-risk inference.*

## Dogs & cats

Spines may injure paws or mouths. No pet-specific chemical toxicity is established here. [P-tauricum](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10934427/)

*Evidence scope: Physical-risk inference.*

## Other animals

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for livestock. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
