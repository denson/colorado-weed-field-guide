# Bittersweet nightshade

*Solanum dulcamara*

**Common weed; not an A/B/C listing in the reviewed state rule.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Poisonous berries and foliage.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Not interchangeable with deadly nightshade (Atropa belladonna), edible tomatoes or other Solanum species. County occurrence remains to be mapped in this guide.

## Recognize

A scrambling perennial with purple flowers, a prominent yellow anther cone and berries that ripen red. Some leaves have small basal lobes. [N-solanum-dulcamara](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/solanum-dulcamara/)

## Plants & habitat

An introduced, spreading vine of moist disturbed places; it can climb over surrounding vegetation. [N-solanum-dulcamara](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/solanum-dulcamara/)

## People

All parts are poisonous, including attractive berries. Ingestion can cause gastrointestinal and neurological illness; contact may irritate skin. [N-solanum-dulcamara](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/solanum-dulcamara/)

## Dogs & cats

NC State flags both dogs and cats. Prevent chewing or berry consumption and seek veterinary advice for exposure. [N-solanum-dulcamara](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/solanum-dulcamara/)

## Other animals

The source also identifies a livestock concern. Do not treat the plant as forage. [N-solanum-dulcamara](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/solanum-dulcamara/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
