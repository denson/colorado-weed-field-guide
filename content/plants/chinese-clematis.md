# Chinese clematis

*Clematis orientalis*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Smothers vegetation; irritating plant compounds.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Native clematis can be difficult to distinguish when not flowering. The toxicology reference treats Clematis broadly; horticultural recommendations for other clematis are not applied to this listed weed.

## Recognize

A perennial vine with compound leaves, yellow bell-shaped flowers and feathery seedheads. [J-chinese-clematis](https://www.jeffco.us/2530/Chinese-Clematis)

## Plants & habitat

Climbs fences, shrubs and trees; heavy coverage can kill supporting vegetation. [J-chinese-clematis](https://www.jeffco.us/2530/Chinese-Clematis)

## People

NC State’s Clematis account reports mouth injury after ingestion and contact dermatitis. Use gloves and avoid tasting. [N-clematis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/clematis/)

*Evidence scope: Genus-level warning, not a C. orientalis exposure series.*

## Dogs & cats

The genus source flags dogs and cats for vomiting, drooling and diarrhea. Keep pets from chewing; species-specific thresholds are unresolved. [N-clematis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/clematis/)

*Evidence scope: Genus-level warning.*

## Other animals

The same genus source flags horses. No safe forage amount is established for Chinese clematis. [N-clematis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/clematis/)

*Evidence scope: Genus-level warning.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
