# Snow-in-summer

*Cerastium tomentosum*

**Introduced garden groundcover.** [S12](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cerastium-tomentosum/) [S11](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/ground-cover-plants/)

> **Spreading habit · Safety gap.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Three views of this plant

Photos are source-identified examples, not a complete identification key. Check leaves, stems, flowers and fruit together. Locations are not assumed to be in Colorado.

{{GALLERY}}

## Recognize

A low mat of woolly, pale foliage and white flowers. Originates in Italy; CSU lists it for hot, dry, sunny groundcover sites. [S12](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cerastium-tomentosum/) [S11](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/ground-cover-plants/)

## Plants & habitat

Spreads by underground runners and seed. CSU describes it as aggressive; garden judgment: keep it from overtaking smaller neighbors. [S11](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/ground-cover-plants/) [S12](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cerastium-tomentosum/)

## People

The sources used for this profile do not establish human ingestion safety. [S11](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/ground-cover-plants/) [S12](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cerastium-tomentosum/)

## Dogs & cats

No species-specific veterinary safety conclusion was established in this review. Do not interpret that gap as pet-safe. [S11](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/ground-cover-plants/) [S12](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cerastium-tomentosum/) [S25](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants)

## Other animals

Flowers attract bees. Safety for grazing animals is not established by the sources reviewed. [S12](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cerastium-tomentosum/)

## Before taking action

Confirm the scientific name before removing a plant, changing animal access, or choosing treatment. An evidence gap means this review has not resolved the question. It does not mean safe.
