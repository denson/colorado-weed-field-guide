# Western ragweed

*Ambrosia psilostachya*

**Native Colorado plant · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **A persistent perennial ragweed; allergy detail still under review.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Unlike annual common and giant ragweeds, this species is perennial. Ambrosia psilostachya is not treated as a synonym of either.

## Identification

A native perennial with relatively slender branching stems and an extensive underground system. Male flower heads occur at the tips; female flowers occur in leaf axils. [F-ambrosia-psilostachya](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/ambpsi)

## People

Ragweeds are associated with allergenic windborne pollen. The reviewed Colorado allergy discussion focuses on common and giant ragweed; western ragweed-specific allergy severity still needs review. [C-ragweed-native](https://sam.extension.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/44/2019/08/August-31-2019-Ragweed.pdf)

*Evidence scope: Related-species context; species-specific evidence unresolved.*

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Other animals

The Forest Service describes forage and wildlife use and does not classify the untreated plant as poisonous in that discussion. It separately reports possible nitrate accumulation in herbicide-treated ragweeds; a safe feeding amount is not established. [F-ambrosia-psilostachya](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/ambpsi)

*Evidence scope: Species forage discussion; broader treated-ragweed warning.*

## Plants & habitat

It occupies native grasslands and disturbed sites and provides food or cover for birds and small mammals. Underground spread can make garden colonies persistent. [F-ambrosia-psilostachya](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/ambpsi)

## Management & timing

Plan follow-up for underground regrowth. Seed prevention alone does not address an established perennial colony. [F-ambrosia-psilostachya](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/ambpsi)

*Evidence scope: Management inference from perennial growth.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
