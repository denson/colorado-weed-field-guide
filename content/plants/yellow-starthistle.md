# Yellow starthistle

*Centaurea solstitialis*

**Colorado noxious weed · List A.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Severe horse poisoning; sharp spines.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** A horse-specific poisoning hazard is established. Do not generalize controlled grazing by other livestock into unrestricted feeding advice.

## Recognize

An annual with yellow flowerheads surrounded by long, radiating spines and gray-green foliage. [N-centaurea-solstitialis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/centaurea-solstitialis/)

## Plants & habitat

Spreads by seed into rangelands and disturbed ground. Prevent seed set and keep contaminated material out of horse feed. [N-centaurea-solstitialis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/centaurea-solstitialis/)

## People

Long flowerhead spines present a puncture hazard; use protective handling. [N-centaurea-solstitialis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/centaurea-solstitialis/)

## Dogs & cats

Spines may injure mouths or paws. Horse poisoning findings do not establish a pet-specific toxic dose. [N-centaurea-solstitialis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/centaurea-solstitialis/)

## Other animals

Ingestion can cause devastating loss of chewing and swallowing function in horses. Suspected exposure warrants veterinary assessment. [N-centaurea-solstitialis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/centaurea-solstitialis/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)

This is a statewide eradication priority. Contact your county weed program promptly for identification and an approved management and disposal plan. [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)
