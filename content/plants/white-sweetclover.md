# White sweetclover

*Melilotus albus*

**Common introduced weed · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Moldy sweetclover hay can cause dangerous bleeding.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Older sources often spell the name Melilotus alba. This is not white clover (Trifolium repens). Sweetclover hay warnings are specific to the documented feed conditions, not a pet dose calculation.

## Identification

Usually a tall biennial with three leaflets and long clusters of small white flowers. Fresh flowers distinguish it from yellow sweetclover; dried flowers may be misleading. [F-melilotus](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/melspp)

## People

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for people. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Dogs & cats

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for dogs or cats. Missing evidence does not establish safety.

## Other animals

Moldy or improperly cured sweetclover hay can cause impaired clotting and fatal bleeding. Diets dominated by sweetclover may also cause bloat. [F-melilotus](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/melspp)

*Evidence scope: Sweetclover group evidence.*

## Plants & habitat

A nonnative legume that supplies forage and pollinator resources but can spread into disturbed and natural vegetation. [F-melilotus](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/melspp)

## Management & timing

Prevent seed production where unwanted and monitor subsequent seedlings. Have suspect hay assessed before feeding. [F-melilotus](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/melspp)

*Evidence scope: Management synthesis.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
