# Plains pricklypear

*Opuntia polyacantha*

**Native Colorado plant · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Sharp spines around paws, mouths and eyes.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Many Opuntia species share prickly-pear names. Source photographs are species references, not a complete key to Colorado cacti or a basis for edible-fruit identification.

## Identification

A low, mat-forming cactus with flattened pads, clusters of spines and fruits that dry at maturity. Several varieties are included within this species. [F-opuntia](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/opupol)

## People

Pads and fruits carry sharp points. Avoid bare-hand handling; eye contact deserves prompt medical assessment. [F-opuntia](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/opupol)

*Evidence scope: Mechanical-injury precaution inferred from spines.*

## Dogs & cats

Keep animals from running into, biting or pawing the cactus. Spines create a mechanical injury risk; chemical ingestion toxicity is not established here. [F-opuntia](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/opupol)

*Evidence scope: Mechanical-injury precaution; toxicology unresolved.*

## Other animals

Some wildlife eat it. Livestock forage use described by the Forest Service does not eliminate the hazard of intact spines. [F-opuntia](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/opupol)

## Plants & habitat

A native of dry grasslands and shrublands and a participant in CSU’s very-low-water demonstration garden. It can be desirable away from heavy foot or pet traffic. [F-opuntia](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/opupol) [C-low-water-cactus](https://elpaso.extension.colostate.edu/native-plant-demonstration-garden/very-low-water-garden/)

## Management & timing

Choose placement with room around walkways and animal routes. Inspect for detached pads before allowing pets back into a worked area. [F-opuntia](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/opupol)

*Evidence scope: Placement and handling precaution.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
