# Rush skeletonweed

*Chondrilla juncea*

**Colorado noxious weed · List A.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Deep roots and fragments sustain spread.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Recognize

A basal rosette gives way to nearly leafless, branching stems with small yellow flowerheads. Lower stems have coarse, downward-pointing hairs. [WA-rush-skeletonweed](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/rush-skeletonweed)

## Plants & habitat

Wind-carried seeds and root buds spread the plant. Deep roots and fragments can regenerate; infestations displace forage and native vegetation. [WA-rush-skeletonweed](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/rush-skeletonweed)
