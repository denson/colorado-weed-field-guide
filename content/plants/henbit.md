## Identification

A cool-season annual with square stems, rounded scalloped leaves and tubular purple-pink flowers. Upper leaves clasp the stem; lower leaves have stalks. [N-lamium-amplexicaule](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/lamium-amplexicaule/)

## Plants & habitat

It can spread in moist disturbed soil by seed and rooting stems. Its early flowers supply nectar and pollen to bees. [N-lamium-amplexicaule](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/lamium-amplexicaule/)

## Management & timing

Where unwanted, remove young plants and rooting stems before seed production. The shallow root system and annual life cycle make seed prevention useful. [N-lamium-amplexicaule](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/lamium-amplexicaule/)
