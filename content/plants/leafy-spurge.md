# Leafy spurge

*Euphorbia esula*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B — Stop the spread.** [J-leafy-spurge](https://www.jeffco.us/2406/Leafy-Spurge)

Established in some areas. Requirements vary by location: eradication, containment or suppression to stop further spread. This is a management category, separate from danger ratings. [Category source](https://fremontcountyco.gov/weed-control/weed-identification) · [What the lists mean]({{BASE}}/safety/#weed-lists).

> **Irritating sap; eye injury risk.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Recognize

Upright stems, narrow leaves and yellow-green bracts surrounding small flowers. All parts contain milky latex. Do not break stems just to test for sap. [N-euphorbia-esula](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-esula/)

## Plants & habitat

Spreads by roots and seed, forming persistent patches that displace vegetation. Root fragments can regrow. [J-leafy-spurge](https://www.jeffco.us/2406/Leafy-Spurge)

## People

Sap can cause dermatitis and potentially permanent eye damage; ingestion can blister the mouth. Protect skin and eyes. [N-euphorbia-esula](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-esula/)

## Dogs & cats

NC State flags both dogs and cats and advises keeping pets away. A pet-specific toxic dose is not provided. [N-euphorbia-esula](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/euphorbia-esula/)

## Other animals

Cattle can suffer lesions and digestive irritation. Managed sheep or goat grazing is a specialist control method, not evidence that unrestricted consumption is safe for all animals. [J-leafy-spurge](https://www.jeffco.us/2406/Leafy-Spurge)
