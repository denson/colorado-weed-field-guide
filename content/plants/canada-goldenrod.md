# Canada goldenrod

*Solidago canadensis*

**Colorado native.** [N-solidago-canadensis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/solidago-canadensis/)

> **Spreads by roots and seed.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Goldenrod taxonomy is variable. Other Solidago species and plants sharing this common name require separate identification.

## Recognize

Late-season, branching sprays of small yellow flowerheads on an upright perennial. [N-solidago-canadensis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/solidago-canadensis/)

## Plants & habitat

Spreads by seed and rhizomes; useful in meadow plantings but may need boundaries in small beds. Flowers attract pollinators and seeds feed birds. [N-solidago-canadensis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/solidago-canadensis/)

## People

Goldenrod is different from wind-pollinated ragweed. That distinction does not guarantee freedom from individual sensitivities or ingestion risk. [N-solidago-canadensis](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/solidago-canadensis/)
