# Wild proso millet

*Panicum miliaceum*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Seed contamination and crop competition.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Cultivated proso and wild proso belong to the same species. These species-reference photographs include cultivated settings and do not demonstrate a wild, shattering or herbicide-resistant biotype. Seed and field-history assessment is needed.

## Recognize

An annual grass with hairy leaf sheaths and a branched panicle that may remain partly enclosed by the upper sheath. [K-wild-proso](https://cals.cornell.edu/weed-science/weed-profiles/wild-proso-millet)

## Plants & habitat

Weedy biotypes compete in crops and spread with contaminated seed or machinery. Prevent seed movement between fields. [K-wild-proso](https://cals.cornell.edu/weed-science/weed-profiles/wild-proso-millet)
