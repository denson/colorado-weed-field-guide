# Siberian elm

*Ulmus pumila*

**Colorado noxious weed · List C — Manage widespread weeds.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

Widespread and well established. Control is recommended by the state; local governments may require management. This is a management category, separate from danger ratings. [Category source](https://fremontcountyco.gov/weed-control/weed-identification) · [What the lists mean]({{BASE}}/safety/#weed-lists).

> **Numerous seedlings; weak branches.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Weak-wood descriptions support the structural concern; this guide cannot assess the stability of an individual tree.

## Recognize

A deciduous tree with small, toothed leaves and round, papery winged fruits in spring. [N-ulmus-pumila](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ulmus-pumila/)

## Plants & habitat

Abundant seeds create colonies in disturbed ground and grasslands; small seedlings are easier to manage than established trees. [N-ulmus-pumila](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ulmus-pumila/)

## People

Weak wood and branches are a structural concern. Have a qualified arborist assess large trees near occupied areas. [N-ulmus-pumila](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ulmus-pumila/)

## Dogs & cats

Falling limbs can endanger animals as well as people; this is a structural-risk inference. Pet ingestion effects are unresolved. [N-ulmus-pumila](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ulmus-pumila/)

## Other animals

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for livestock. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
