# Sulfur cinquefoil

*Potentilla recta*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Persistent pasture and roadside weed.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Colorado has native cinquefoils. Confirm Potentilla recta before removing a yellow-flowered cinquefoil.

## Recognize

A hairy perennial with pale yellow, five-petaled flowers and leaves divided into toothed leaflets. [N-potentilla-recta](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/potentilla-recta/)

## Plants & habitat

Self-seeds in pastures, disturbed ground and roadsides. Persistent seeds require follow-up after removal. [N-potentilla-recta](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/potentilla-recta/)
