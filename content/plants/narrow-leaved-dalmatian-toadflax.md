# Narrow-leaved Dalmatian toadflax

*Linaria genistifolia*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Listed taxon with difficult identification.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Colorado’s listing name is retained. Drought-stressed L. dalmatica and hybrid toadflaxes can also have narrow leaves. Photographs from Europe show source-identified L. genistifolia; they do not confirm Colorado occurrence. Expert identification is needed before assigning a local plant to this taxon.

## Recognize

The federal guide distinguishes L. genistifolia by smaller flowers and capsules; narrow leaves alone are unreliable. [F-toadflaxes](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-A13-PURL-gpo87594/pdf/GOVPUB-A13-PURL-gpo87594.pdf)

## Plants & habitat

Colorado lists this taxon separately. The 2016 federal guide considered its North American distribution unconfirmed, so a state listing is not proof of a local population. [F-toadflaxes](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-A13-PURL-gpo87594/pdf/GOVPUB-A13-PURL-gpo87594.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

*Evidence scope: Regulatory listing and historical taxonomic uncertainty.*
