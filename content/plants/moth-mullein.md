# Moth mullein

*Verbascum blattaria*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Long-lived seeds sustain infestations.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Unlike common mullein, this species does not have a thickly woolly rosette. Confirm the whole plant, not flower color alone.

## Recognize

A biennial with toothed leaves and loose stalks of white or yellow flowers with purple centers. [J-moth-mullein](https://www.jeffco.us/2402/Moth-Mullein)

## Plants & habitat

A garden escape in pastures and hay fields. Seeds persist, requiring monitoring after removal. [J-moth-mullein](https://www.jeffco.us/2402/Moth-Mullein)
