# Orange hawkweed

*Pilosella aurantiaca*

**Colorado noxious weed · List A.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Runners crowd lawns and mountain meadows.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Colorado lists the synonym Hieracium aurantiacum. The name orange paintbrush must not be confused with native Castilleja species.

## Recognize

Clusters of vivid orange-red flowerheads rise over hairy basal leaves; flowering stems carry few leaves. [WA-orange-hawkweed](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/orange-hawkweed)

## Plants & habitat

Seed, surface runners and rhizomes produce spreading patches that crowd out desirable forage and meadow plants. [WA-orange-hawkweed](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/orange-hawkweed)
