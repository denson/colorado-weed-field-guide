# Annual sowthistle

*Sonchus oleraceus*

**Common introduced weed · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Windborne seed and crop-pest hosting.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Separate from prickly sowthistle (Sonchus asper) and the root-spreading perennial sowthistle (Sonchus arvensis). Seedlings can be difficult to separate.

## Identification

An upright annual with hollow stems, milky sap, yellow flower heads and fluffy seedheads. Upper leaves clasp the stem with pointed, clawlike basal lobes. [U-sowthistle](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/weeds-identification-gallery/annual-sowthistle/)

## Plants & habitat

It occupies disturbed gardens and cropland and can host aphids and other crop pests. The cited examples are from California; they do not prove a particular Colorado pest outbreak. [U-sowthistle](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/weeds-identification-gallery/annual-sowthistle/)

*Evidence scope: Species ecology; location-limited pest examples.*

## Management & timing

Prevent plants from producing and dispersing seed. Confirm the species before treating a recurring colony as annual sowthistle. [U-sowthistle](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/weeds-identification-gallery/annual-sowthistle/)

*Evidence scope: Management inference from annual seed reproduction.*
