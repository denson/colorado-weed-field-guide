# Poison hemlock

*Conium maculatum*

**Colorado List C.** [S16](https://www.jeffco.us/2230/Poison-Hemlock)

> **Poisonous to people & animals.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Three views of this plant

Photos are source-identified examples, not a complete identification key. Check leaves, stems, flowers and fruit together. Locations are not assumed to be in Colorado.

{{GALLERY}}

## Recognize

Finely divided leaves, stems with purple spots, and white umbrella-like flower clusters. Often near streams, ditches, and pasture edges. [S16](https://www.jeffco.us/2230/Poison-Hemlock)

## Plants & habitat

An introduced, colony-forming biennial. Garden judgment: exclude access and seek local management help once identification is confirmed. [S16](https://www.jeffco.us/2230/Poison-Hemlock)

## People

All parts are poisonous. Do not eat or taste it to identify it. Suspected ingestion warrants immediate Poison Control advice. [S16](https://www.jeffco.us/2230/Poison-Hemlock) [S26](https://www.poison.org/first-aid-for-poisonings)

## Dogs & cats

The county warning covers animals broadly. Treat suspected ingestion by a pet as urgent and contact a veterinarian. [S16](https://www.jeffco.us/2230/Poison-Hemlock) [S04](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/milkweed)

## Other animals

Poisonous to livestock; do not use grazing as a control method. A List C designation does not indicate low toxicity. [S16](https://www.jeffco.us/2230/Poison-Hemlock)

## Before taking action

Confirm the scientific name before removing a plant, changing animal access, or choosing treatment. An evidence gap means this review has not resolved the question. It does not mean safe. For listed weeds, consult your county weed program about the requirements for your location; follow the current label for any pesticide.
