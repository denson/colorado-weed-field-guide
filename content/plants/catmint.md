# Catmint

*Nepeta × faassenii*

**Introduced garden hybrid.** [N-nepeta-x-faassenii](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/nepeta-x-faassenii/)

> **Spreading clumps; pet evidence gap.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Sterility is reported for this hybrid, not all plants sold as catmint. Verify cultivar and scientific name.

## Recognize

Gray-green aromatic leaves, square stems and lavender-blue flowers. [N-nepeta-x-faassenii](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/nepeta-x-faassenii/)

## Plants & habitat

A drought-tolerant, sterile hybrid that expands as a clump; it is unlikely to reseed. Divide or trim when it outgrows its space. [N-nepeta-x-faassenii](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/nepeta-x-faassenii/)

## People

The horticultural source does not provide a human ingestion assessment. This is an evidence gap, not proof of safety.

## Dogs & cats

Its scent can attract cats. This does not establish ingestion safety. Catnip (Nepeta cataria) is a different taxon; its poison listing cannot automatically classify this hybrid. [N-nepeta-x-faassenii](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/nepeta-x-faassenii/)
