# Dalmatian toadflax

*Linaria dalmatica*

**Colorado noxious weed · List B — Stop the spread.** [J-dalmatian-toadflax](https://www.jeffco.us/2469/Dalmatian-Toadflax)

Established in some areas. Requirements vary by location: eradication, containment or suppression to stop further spread. This is a management category, separate from danger ratings. [Category source](https://fremontcountyco.gov/weed-control/weed-identification) · [What the lists mean]({{BASE}}/safety/#weed-lists).

> **Spreads from roots; livestock concern.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Distinguish the two toadflaxes by leaves as well as flowers. A snapdragon-like bloom is not evidence of garden suitability.

## Recognize

Yellow snapdragon-like flowers with long spurs; broad, waxy, heart-shaped leaves clasp the stems. [J-dalmatian-toadflax](https://www.jeffco.us/2469/Dalmatian-Toadflax)

## Plants & habitat

A perennial spreading by roots and seed. Root fragments can form new plants; repeated casual digging or mowing is unlikely to solve an established patch. [J-dalmatian-toadflax](https://www.jeffco.us/2469/Dalmatian-Toadflax)
