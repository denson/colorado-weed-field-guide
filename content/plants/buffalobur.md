# Buffalobur

*Solanum rostratum*

**Great Plains native; documented in Colorado.** [I-buffalobur](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/encyclopedia/buffalobur) [U-buffalobur](https://botany-server3.colorado.edu/collections/list.php?page=3&taxa=2365&taxontype=2&usethes=1)

> **Sharp spines; toxic to livestock.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Washington lists this plant as a noxious weed in that state. That legal designation is not transferred to Colorado.

## Three views of this plant

Photos are source-identified examples, not a complete identification key. Check leaves, stems, flowers and fruit together. Locations are not assumed to be in Colorado.

## Recognize

Yellow, five-lobed flowers; deeply lobed leaves with conspicuous spines on the veins and stems. This is an annual. [I-buffalobur](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/encyclopedia/buffalobur)

## Plants & habitat

Can compete strongly with forage in disturbed pastures. Prevent seed production where removal is needed. [W-buffalobur](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/buffalobur)

## People

The abundant spines present a puncture hazard. Use sturdy gloves for removal; this is a physical-risk inference from the documented spines. Human ingestion effects are not established here. [I-buffalobur](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/encyclopedia/buffalobur)

*Evidence scope: Documented spines; editorial risk inference.*

## Dogs & cats

Spines may injure paws or mouths. Pet-specific chemical toxicity is not established by the livestock source; prevent chewing. [W-buffalobur](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/buffalobur)

*Evidence scope: Physical-risk inference; pet evidence gap.*

## Other animals

Reported toxic to livestock; burs also catch in sheep wool. [W-buffalobur](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/buffalobur)

## Before taking action

Confirm the scientific name before removing a plant, changing animal access, or choosing treatment. An evidence gap means this review has not resolved the question. It does not mean safe.
