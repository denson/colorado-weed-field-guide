# Russian thistle

*Salsola tragus*

**Introduced annual weed; not designated on the reviewed Colorado A/B/C lists.** [C-tumbleweeds](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/identification-and-management-of-kochia-and-russian-thistle/) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Prickly tumbleweeds; livestock and fire hazards.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Russian thistle is a common name for several similar Salsola taxa. This profile uses S. tragus as its principal name; UC’s identification and dry-plant hazards address a species complex. A round tumbleweed alone does not establish the species.

## Recognize

A branching annual that becomes rounded and prickly. Mature leaves and bracts have stiff tips; stems often show reddish stripes. Dry plants can detach and tumble. [UC-russian-thistle](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/weeds-identification-gallery/russian-thistle/)

*Evidence scope: Russian-thistle species complex.*

## Plants & habitat

Invades disturbed ground. Dry accumulations can obstruct traffic and create a fire hazard; prevent seed production before plants detach. [UC-russian-thistle](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/weeds-identification-gallery/russian-thistle/) [C-tumbleweeds](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/identification-and-management-of-kochia-and-russian-thistle/)

*Evidence scope: Species-group hazards; editorial prevention priority.*

## People

Stiff, sharp parts can puncture skin. Wear sturdy gloves when handling dry plants; this is a physical-risk inference, not a human poison diagnosis. [UC-russian-thistle](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/weeds-identification-gallery/russian-thistle/)

*Evidence scope: Physical-risk inference.*

## Dogs & cats

Prickly material may injure paws or mouths. The reviewed livestock findings do not establish a pet toxic dose. Keep pets away from dry piles. [UC-russian-thistle](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/weeds-identification-gallery/russian-thistle/)

*Evidence scope: Physical-risk inference; pet toxicology gap.*

## Other animals

CSU reports nitrate and oxalate poisoning risks for cattle and sheep, including severe breathing problems or kidney damage. Do not assume this weed is suitable forage. [C-tumbleweeds](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/identification-and-management-of-kochia-and-russian-thistle/)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
