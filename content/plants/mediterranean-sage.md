# Mediterranean sage

*Salvia aethiopis*

**Colorado noxious weed · List A — Eradicate.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

Uncommon in Colorado. All populations are designated for eradication statewide. This is a management category, separate from danger ratings. [Category source](https://fremontcountyco.gov/weed-control/weed-identification) · [What the lists mean]({{BASE}}/safety/#weed-lists).

> **Tumbles and spreads seed through rangeland.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** This is another tumbleweed-forming plant, distinct from Russian sage (Salvia yangii), kochia and Russian thistle. Do not merge their toxicity evidence.

## Recognize

A woolly rosette produces a much-branched, candelabra-like stalk with pale, two-lipped flowers. Upper leaves clasp the square stem. [WA-mediterranean-sage](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/mediterranean-sage)

## Plants & habitat

Unpalatable growth can displace forage. Dry flowering stalks detach and tumble, spreading seed. [WA-mediterranean-sage](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/mediterranean-sage)
