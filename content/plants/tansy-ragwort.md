# Tansy ragwort

*Jacobaea vulgaris*

**Colorado noxious weed · List A.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Poisonous to livestock; harmful alkaloids.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** This is not common tansy or common groundsel. The attempted NC State Jacobaea URL returned a common-groundsel account and was excluded as mismatched evidence.

## Recognize

Ruffled, divided leaves and clusters of yellow daisy-like flowerheads, typically with about thirteen ray flowers. [WA-tansy-ragwort](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/tansy-ragwort)

## Plants & habitat

Seed and regrowth spread plants in disturbed fields and pastures. Mowing alone can produce repeated regrowth. [WA-tansy-ragwort](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/tansy-ragwort)

## People

All parts contain toxic alkaloids. Do not use the plant in food or home remedies; direct human exposure thresholds are not established here. [WA-tansy-ragwort](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/tansy-ragwort)

## Dogs & cats

ASPCA lists Senecio species as toxic to dogs and cats, with liver injury from pyrrolizidine alkaloids. Tansy ragwort was formerly Senecio jacobaea; the ASPCA warning is broader than this species and gives no pet-specific dose. [A-ragwort](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/ragwort) [WA-tansy-ragwort](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/tansy-ragwort)

*Evidence scope: Historical genus-group warning; species-specific alkaloid evidence.*

## Other animals

All parts are poisonous to livestock. Removed or wilted plants can be more palatable; keep cut material and contaminated forage away from animals. [WA-tansy-ragwort](https://www.nwcb.wa.gov/weeds/tansy-ragwort)

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)

This is a statewide eradication priority. Contact your county weed program promptly for identification and an approved management and disposal plan. [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)
