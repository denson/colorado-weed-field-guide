## Recognize

A woody shrub or tree with narrow, silvery leaves, fragrant yellow flowers and olive-shaped fruits. Branches may bear long thorns. [J-russian-olive](https://www.jeffco.us/2224/Russian-Olive)

## Plants & habitat

An introduced plant of riparian areas and old windbreaks. Stumps, roots and discarded twigs can regrow; plan removal and follow-up together. [J-russian-olive](https://www.jeffco.us/2224/Russian-Olive)

## People

Thorns can puncture skin and eyes. Protective handling is a physical-risk precaution; this profile does not assess fruit consumption. [J-russian-olive](https://www.jeffco.us/2224/Russian-Olive)

*Evidence scope: Physical-risk inference.*

## Dogs & cats

Thorny branches can injure animals. Pet-specific chemical toxicity is not established in this review. [J-russian-olive](https://www.jeffco.us/2224/Russian-Olive)

*Evidence scope: Physical-risk inference; toxicology gap.*
