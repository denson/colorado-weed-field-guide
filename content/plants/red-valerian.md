# Red valerian

*Centranthus ruber*

**Introduced drought-tolerant ornamental.** [N-centranthus-ruber](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/centranthus-ruber/)

> **Self-seeds beyond garden beds.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** The retrieved NC State source now treats Centranthus ruber as Valeriana rubra. The familiar garden name is retained for discovery; it is not Valeriana officinalis.

## Recognize

Clusters of small red, pink or white flowers above opposite, fleshy leaves. [N-centranthus-ruber](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/centranthus-ruber/)

## Plants & habitat

Wind-dispersed seed can naturalize beyond a garden. Deadheading reduces spread; drought tolerance is not evidence that a plant is ecologically suitable everywhere. [N-centranthus-ruber](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/centranthus-ruber/)
