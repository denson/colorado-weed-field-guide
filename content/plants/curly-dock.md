# Curly dock

*Rumex crispus*

**Common weed; not an A/B/C listing in the reviewed state rule.** [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Soluble oxalates; persistent roots and seeds.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Recognize

A basal rosette and upright stems with long, wavy-edged leaves; green flower clusters mature to brown seed structures. [N-rumex-crispus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/rumex-crispus/)

## Plants & habitat

A Colorado weed of disturbed land. Its long-lived root and persistent seeds allow survival through mowing and changing moisture. [C-descriptions](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/weed-descriptions/) [N-rumex-crispus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/rumex-crispus/)

## People

The reviewed sources do not establish species-specific ingestion effects or a safe exposure amount for people. Missing evidence does not establish safety. 

## Dogs & cats

NC State flags dogs and cats and reports soluble oxalates, salivation, tremors and possible kidney injury; it describes kidney failure as rare in these pets. [N-rumex-crispus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/rumex-crispus/)

## Other animals

The source flags horses, but does not establish a safe forage quantity. [N-rumex-crispus](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/rumex-crispus/)

*Evidence scope: Source hazard flag; limited horse clinical detail.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
