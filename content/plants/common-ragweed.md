# Common ragweed

*Ambrosia artemisiifolia*

**Colorado native.** [N-ambrosia-artemisiifolia](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ambrosia-artemisiifolia/)

> **Pollen allergy; skin irritation.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Three views of this plant

Photos are source-identified examples, not a complete identification key. Check leaves, stems, flowers and fruit together. Locations are not assumed to be in Colorado.

{{GALLERY}}

## Recognize

An annual with deeply divided, fern-like leaves and inconspicuous green flower spikes. [N-ambrosia-artemisiifolia](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ambrosia-artemisiifolia/)

## Plants & habitat

A dry-site volunteer that produces many persistent seeds; birds eat its seeds. Remove unwanted plants before flowering and seed set. [N-ambrosia-artemisiifolia](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ambrosia-artemisiifolia/)

## People

Windborne pollen is a major cause of hay fever; contact dermatitis is also reported. [N-ambrosia-artemisiifolia](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ambrosia-artemisiifolia/)
