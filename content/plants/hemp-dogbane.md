# Hemp dogbane

*Apocynum cannabinum*

**Native Colorado plant · not on state Lists A/B/C.** [C-floristic-2020](https://cnhp.colostate.edu/download/documents/2020/CO_FQA_2020_Final_Report.pdf) [CO-rules-2025](https://www.sos.state.co.us/CCR/GenerateRulePdf.do?ruleVersionId=11948&fileName=8+CCR+1206-2)

> **Cardiac-glycoside poisoning hazard for people and animals.** [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

**Identification caveat:** Not Cannabis. Apocynum androsaemifolium is a different dogbane. Pet signs are explicitly sourced to the ASPCA genus account, not silently transferred from its differently named species page.

## Identification

A branching perennial with opposite oval leaves, milky sap, small greenish-white flowers and narrow paired pods containing silky-haired seed. [N-apocynum-cannabinum](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/apocynum-cannabinum/)

## People

All parts are highly toxic if ingested. Reported effects include gastrointestinal illness, weakness and severe heart effects; sap can also blister skin. [N-apocynum-cannabinum](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/apocynum-cannabinum/)

## Dogs & cats

ASPCA lists Apocynum species as toxic to dogs and cats. Its group-level signs include weakness, diarrhea and a slow heart rate. Contact a veterinarian promptly after suspected ingestion. [P-dogbane](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/dogbane-hemp)

*Evidence scope: Genus-level veterinary evidence.*

## Other animals

The Forest Service reports livestock poisoning, including potentially fatal effects. Low palatability does not make contaminated forage safe. [F-apocynum](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/apocan)

## Plants & habitat

It spreads underground and can form persistent colonies. It also supplies nectar and habitat for insects. Native status and ecological value coexist with a serious ingestion hazard. [N-apocynum-cannabinum](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/apocynum-cannabinum/)

## Management & timing

Plan control of the whole colony and prevent access by grazing or chewing animals. Protect skin from sap while handling. [N-apocynum-cannabinum](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/apocynum-cannabinum/) [F-apocynum](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/apocan)

*Evidence scope: Management synthesis.*

## Before taking action

Confirm identification and the requirements of your county weed program. For chemical treatment, follow the current product label, including restrictions around animals and water. [S27](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2103-colorado-noxious-weeds/)
