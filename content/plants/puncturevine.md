# Puncturevine / goathead

*Tribulus terrestris*

**Colorado List C.** [S29](https://www.larimer.gov/sites/default/files/uploads/2023/weed_management_reference_guide_-_5.5_x_8_-_jan_24_2023_-_website_reader_spreads_0.pdf)

> **Sharp burs · Animal injury.** Native status, garden use and noxious-weed class are separate from toxicity. [Exposure help]({{BASE}}/safety/).

## Three views of this plant

Photos are source-identified examples, not a complete identification key. Check leaves, stems, flowers and fruit together. Locations are not assumed to be in Colorado.

{{GALLERY}}

## Recognize

Low, radiating stems with small leaflets, yellow flowers, and hard, spiny burs. Common on disturbed ground. [S17](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2109-puncture-vine/)

## Plants & habitat

Can form spreading mats and compete with other plants. Preventing seed production is central to management. [S18](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/puncturevine/)

## People

Spiny burs puncture skin and can remain a hazard after the plant dies. Use protective gloves when handling. [S17](https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/weeds-cultural-problems/2109-puncture-vine/) [S18](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/puncturevine/)

## Dogs & cats

Burs can injure animals. Pet precaution: keep play areas clear of burs and check paws after contact. [S18](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/puncturevine/) [S19](https://pueblo.extension.colostate.edu/here-they-come/)

## Other animals

Burs can injure grazing animals’ mouths. Puncturevine can also be toxic to sheep; livestock findings do not establish a pet toxicity dose. [S18](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/puncturevine/)

## Before taking action

Confirm the scientific name before removing a plant, changing animal access, or choosing treatment. An evidence gap means this review has not resolved the question. It does not mean safe. For listed weeds, consult your county weed program about the requirements for your location; follow the current label for any pesticide.
